# Patent Research Notes - v0.1 - 2026-09-03

## Purpose

This note records the external legal/technical sources used in the current §112(a) drafting pass. It is not a substitute for the tracker Source Inventory or patent-counsel review.

## Current MPEP drafting controls

- MPEP §2163, Written Description:
  https://www.uspto.gov/web/offices/pac/mpep/s2163.html
  - The filing must convey possession of the claimed invention.
  - Broad genus support may require a representative number of species or relevant common identifying characteristics.
  - Original claims do not cure result-only disclosure if the specification does not sufficiently identify how the function/result is achieved.

- MPEP §2164, Enablement:
  https://www.uspto.gov/web/offices/pac/mpep/s2164.html
  - Enablement must extend to the scope pursued without undue experimentation.
  - Claim breadth, guidance, examples, predictability, and the amount of experimentation are relevant.

- MPEP §608.01(a), Arrangement of Application:
  https://www.uspto.gov/web/offices/pac/mpep/s608.html
  - Current working patent follows the preferred utility-application specification organization.

## Technical sources applied in this pass

### Reproductive control

USDA APHIS NWRC - Population Management / Reproductive Control:
https://www.aphis.usda.gov/national-wildlife-programs/nwrc/research-areas/reproductive-control

Current USDA materials identify representative wildlife fertility-control technologies including GnRH/GonaCon, PZP, and diazacon. USDA reports GonaCon research in domestic and feral swine and also identifies oral/nasal GnRH delivery research objectives. These materials provide concrete public-art context; they do not establish that the inventors conceived every route or formulation.

### Sodium nitrite / differential susceptibility

Bait Preference of Free-Ranging Feral Swine for Delivery of a Novel Toxicant:
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4727921/

The literature supports sodium nitrite as a feral-swine toxicant research example and recognizes non-target risk. The present draft therefore treats sodium nitrite as a predetermined-toxicant / differential-susceptibility example, not as proof of exclusive feral-pig species specificity.

### KAPUT / warfarin

EPA product page:
https://ordspub.epa.gov/ords/pesticides/f?p=PPLS:8:::NO::P8_PUID:519508

EPA accepted label:
https://www3.epa.gov/pesticides/chem_search/ppls/072500-00026-20180405.pdf

EPA identifies KAPUT FERAL HOG BAIT under EPA Registration No. 72500-26 and identifies warfarin 0.005% as active ingredient on the accepted label. The patent draft uses the trade name together with the technical/regulatory identification. Current label/status must be reverified before any deployment statement.

### Genetic biocontrol

Manipulating the Destiny of Wild Populations Using CRISPR:
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11064769/

This review provides public-art background for genetic-biocontrol concepts such as population suppression, population modification, fertility effects, inheritance effects, and sex-ratio strategies. It does not establish inventor possession of a particular feral-swine genetic-control embodiment. The current genetic-control claim family remains inventor-confirmation and enablement gated.

## Drafting consequence

External literature may show that a technical class exists and can help identify the state of the art. It does not substitute for the inventors' conception or the application's own written description. The filing copy should retain only those broad branches for which inventor-confirmed disclosure can credibly support the intended later claim scope.
