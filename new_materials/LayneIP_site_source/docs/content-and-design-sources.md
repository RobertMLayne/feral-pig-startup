# Content and design record

Reviewed September 21, 2026. The website is an original design for Layne Intellectual Property; no other firm's copy, logo, photography, results, rankings or client lists have been reused.

The visual direction preserves the existing Layne navy/teal identity and uses self-hosted serif and sans-serif typography, a restrained copper mark, large editorial headings, readable service detail, and consistent navigation. The directory has one actual professional today; its data model and filters can grow with real additions.

## Personal factual basis

The owner's supplied `2026-02-26_CV.docx` supports the professional name, degrees, selected employment history, U.S. patent-agent registration number 82,283, professional email, phone, and broad technical background. The site does not publish the residential street address, unrelated personal information, or claims about specific client matters/results.

Registration is stated from the owner's supplied professional record. **Current active registration was not independently confirmed through a live OED roster**; check current status as part of public launch. Professional email and phone are sourced from the CV; actual mailbox and telephone delivery have not been tested.

Past employment is presented as history, not a current affiliation or endorsement. No assistant-authored case victory, testimonial, ranking, client count, patent count, office location or additional employee appears on the site. Broad service offerings describe potential engagement scope, not a claim that every listed procedure has previously been handled.

## Patent-agent scope and procedural resources

- [USPTO MPEP § 401](https://www.uspto.gov/web/offices/pac/mpep/s401.html) and [§ 402](https://www.uspto.gov/web/offices/pac/mpep/s402.html): representation and recognition.
- [USPTO: applying for a patent](https://www.uspto.gov/patents/basics/apply) and [provisional applications](https://www.uspto.gov/patents/basics/apply/provisional-application).
- [USPTO MPEP § 714](https://www.uspto.gov/web/offices/pac/mpep/s714.html): amendments and applicant action.
- [USPTO Patent Cooperation Treaty guidance](https://www.uspto.gov/patents/basics/international-protection/patent-cooperation-treaty).

Service pages link to relevant primary USPTO resources. Court advocacy, licensing agreements, freedom-to-operate opinions and other legal work outside authorized patent-agent practice are identified as attorney work; technical support for counsel is described separately. Foreign prosecution requires appropriately qualified local practitioners. Procedure-specific eligibility, competence, actual record and deadlines must be assessed for an accepted matter.

No fee schedule, result guarantee, confidential intake service, client portal, secure upload server or automated legal advice is implied. The contact flow prepares a user-reviewed email; the evidence utility processes its inputs locally.
