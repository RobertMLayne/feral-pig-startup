Reissue & ex parte reexamination

Evaluate selected patent-office mechanisms for addressing issues in an issued patent.

LAYNE / IP
← All services

Begin with the issued claims, the full record, and a precise objective.

An issued patent presents a different procedural setting from a pending original application. Reissue and ex parte reexamination serve distinct purposes, and neither should be selected without examining eligibility, scope, and consequences.

Scoped patent-office assistance can include technical and record analysis, preparation of supporting materials, and prosecution work where accepted. Related litigation and transaction questions are coordinated with appropriate counsel.

What the work can include

  • Issued-claim and prosecution-history review
  • Technical prior-art mapping
  • Reissue issue identification and application support
  • Ex parte reexamination request or response support
  • Evidence organization and citation verification
  • Coordination with litigation counsel where relevant

Typical work product

  • Technical record and issue matrix
  • Source-supported draft materials
  • Questions for coordinated procedural review

Engagement scope

These are matter-specific services, subject to suitability and capacity review. No court representation or outcome is implied.
