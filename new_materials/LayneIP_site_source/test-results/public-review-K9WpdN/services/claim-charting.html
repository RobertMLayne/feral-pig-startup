Claim charts & technical litigation support

Connect each claim limitation to inspectable technical evidence for review by litigation counsel.

LAYNE / IP
← All services

A conclusion is only as useful as the evidence that supports it.

Technical litigation work often turns on the connection between claim language and the operation of a real system. A useful analysis identifies the source, its version, the relevant passage or artifact, and the reasoning that connects it to a limitation.

Support can include infringement and invalidity chart preparation, source-code or technical-document review, expert-report support, and claim-construction research. Litigation strategy, legal opinions, filings, and court advocacy remain with the responsible attorneys.

What the work can include

  • Limitation-level infringement and invalidity charts
  • Software and technical-document review
  • Android APK/DEX and control-flow analysis
  • Semiconductor and wireless technical analysis
  • Expert-report and deposition preparation support
  • Evidence provenance and open-issue tracking

Typical work product

  • Traceable claim charts
  • Technical memoranda and annotated evidence
  • Unresolved questions and verification plan

Engagement scope

Provided as scientific and technical support to counsel. Layne Intellectual Property does not offer attorney representation in court.

Who this supports

Law firms, supervising experts, and in-house counsel handling technically demanding disputes.
