Continuations & portfolio development

Connect pending claims, disclosed subject matter, and evolving technical priorities across an application family.

LAYNE / IP
← All services

Treat each filing as part of a considered patent record.

Portfolio development requires understanding what has been disclosed, what has been claimed, and how pending applications relate to one another. A structured family map makes those relationships easier to evaluate.

Work focuses on prosecution-related analysis: potential continuation or divisional claims, priority-support questions, prosecution history, and technical coverage relative to the applicant’s objectives. Business and transaction advice is coordinated with appropriate counsel.

What the work can include

  • Application-family and claim-set mapping
  • Continuation and divisional planning
  • Claim coverage relative to disclosed implementations
  • Priority and written-description support review
  • Prosecution history and consistency analysis
  • Technical portfolio triage

Typical work product

  • Family and priority map
  • Claim-coverage matrix
  • Prosecution options with recorded assumptions

Engagement scope

Continuation eligibility and timing depend on the specific family history and pending applications. No benefit claim is assumed without record review.

Who this supports

Applicants maintaining a developing patent family and counsel seeking technical portfolio support.
