LAYNE / IP

A practice built around the work.

Led by Robert M. Layne, Ph.D., the practice supports inventors, research organizations, businesses, and counsel with patent preparation and prosecution, technical evidence, and research workflows.

The approach starts with an explicit question and an inspectable record. What does the technology do? Which evidence establishes the relevant facts? Where are the uncertainties? What work product will help the next reviewer make a decision?

How an engagement develops

  1. Define the question

    Identify the parties, the general technology, the procedural context, and known dates.

  2. Agree on the work

    Confirm conflicts, competence, scope, responsibilities, fees, and the method for exchanging information.

  3. Build the record

    Collect the relevant materials, document assumptions, and develop the agreed analysis or draft.

  4. Review and refine

    Test the work against its sources, resolve questions, and deliver a clear record of the result.

A collaborative role

Patent-agent work is paired with scientific and technical support where that is the right fit. Litigation and transactional legal work remain with responsible attorneys. Foreign patent work is coordinated with appropriately qualified local practitioners.
