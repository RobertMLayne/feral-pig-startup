Ex parte appeals & patent-office petitions

Develop a focused patent-office record when continued examination calls for a different procedural path.

LAYNE / IP
← All services

Match the technical issue to the appropriate patent-office procedure.

When prosecution reaches an impasse, the first task is to identify the issue, the available record, and the procedural route that can address it. The analysis should distinguish an appealable rejection from a matter addressed through a petition.

Scoped assistance can include ex parte appeal analysis, brief preparation, technical support for argument, and patent-office petitions. Engagement depends on the issue, competence, capacity, deadlines, and the existing representation.

What the work can include

  • Ex parte appeal feasibility and issue review
  • Claim, evidence, and rejection mapping
  • Appeal and reply brief preparation
  • Technical argument development
  • Patent-office petition preparation within the agreed scope
  • Coordination with existing patent counsel

Typical work product

  • Procedural options and issue outline
  • Citation-supported draft submissions
  • Record and evidence index

Engagement scope

This service concerns proceedings before the USPTO. Judicial appeals and court representation require an attorney authorized to handle them.

Applicants and prosecution counsel evaluating a patent-office appeal or petition.
